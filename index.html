01 — About the Game
WHAT IS
ARCHIVD?
// game.description

ARCHIVD is a tag-based game — but with a twist.

It's a world where forgotten characters end up, each carrying pieces of their past — old items, memories, and identities tied to archived Roblox content.

The world feels abandoned, but not empty. Everything here was left behind on purpose.

Every character you encounter was once something more — popular, ignored, or lost — but now, they all exist here.

02 — Core Gameplay
HOW IT
WORKS

ARCHIVD plays like tag — but strategy matters.

Players take on the role of archived characters, each with unique abilities tied to their history. Winning isn't just about tagging — it's about understanding who you are inside the archive.

Speed VarianceSome characters move faster. Use that advantage or prepare to be caught.
Stealth & TrackingSome are harder to detect. Others can read patterns through walls of data.
Past-Linked SkillsEvery ability reflects the character's archived identity and history.
Survival StrategyKnow your character. Use their strengths. Outlast the forgotten world.
